Connect Twitch OAuth (One-Time Setup)

To let users log in with Twitch and post alarms directly into their own Twitch chat, register a free Twitch Application in the Twitch Developer Console:

  1. Open dev.twitch.tv/console/apps and click Register Your Application.
  2. Set OAuth Redirect URLs to:
    http://localhost:3000/auth/twitch/callback
  3. Copy your Client ID and generate a Client Secret, then paste them into .env as TWITCH_CLIENT_ID and TWITCH_CLIENT_SECRET and restart the server.

Note: WizeBot is not required — Twitch's official Helix API posts directly into the logged-in Twitch user's channel chat. (WizeBot Custom Events are also supported as an optional add-on in Settings.)
